한국어

  • Google 계정 권한(YouTube 업로드·관리, Google 포토)은 소유자가 직접 만든 영상과 사진을 소유자 본인의 채널과 라이브러리에 올리고 정리하는 데에만 사용합니다.

English

  • This app is a personal tool used only by its owner.
  • Google API access (YouTube upload/manage, Google Photos) is used solely to upload and organize the owner's own videos and photos to the owner's own channel and library.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never sent to other servers or shared with third parties.
  • No data from other users is collected, stored, or sold. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.
